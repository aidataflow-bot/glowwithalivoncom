About Alicia

A mission rooted in real life.

Hi, I’m Alicia Von — a mom, business owner, martial arts leader, and wellness advocate. I created Glow with Alivon to help busy moms and dads improve their health, gain confidence, build healthy habits, and realize they do not have to choose between being a great parent and pursuing their dreams.

Through wellness, nutrition, skincare, beauty, fitness challenges, and entrepreneurship, I help people create lasting transformation from the inside out.
